Lots of experience on M32 and X32. 

They work well and everyone knows how to use them. 

The m32 has better build quality (seems like the encoders, faders, and buttons last longer) even though the x/m are essentially the same board. 

However if choosing between the 3 i believe the Wing has a lot more value in terms of effects, more versatility in how it's set up, and it will be supported longer since it came out later.

The preamp on M is made by Midas, x32's are only designed by Midas

Other stuff is same including DSP and stuff

Also M's screen is angled, useful for watching shit while sitting, i don't think it's possible with X

Wing has best of both with Midas preamps and 5x powerful DSP compared to X&M

M32 with doghouse case on wheels isn’t small, same with the wing. X32 with a normal case isn’t bad and the compact is even easier to move. Personally I’d used the m32r, nice layout, better parts. I mix with an iPad 95% of the time so I don’t care anyway
