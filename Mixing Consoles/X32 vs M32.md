Lots of experience on M32 and X32. They work well and everyone knows how to use them. The m32 has better build quality (seems like the encoders, faders, and buttons last longer) even though the x/m are essentially the same board. However if choosing between the 3 i believe the Wing has a lot more value in terms of effects, more versatility in how it's set up, and it will be supported longer since it came out later.
