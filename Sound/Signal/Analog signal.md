**Analog signal** is an **electrical signal** that reflects the original [[Sound]] wave, and can be carried through the wire.

Where the original [[Sound]] was loud, the electrical current will have high voltage, whereas quiet will have lower AC voltage.

When you add a [[Phantom Power]] (DC), your Analog signal will work on top of that DC stream. It's like the train going in one direction, while someone inside (sound) is constantly swinging back and forth in the hammock.

Another example is to compare car going at the constant speed (DC) forward, while at the same time it's swerving left and right (AC).
